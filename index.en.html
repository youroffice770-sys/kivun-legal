Who built Kivun

Kivun wasn't built in a lab. It came out of 7 years of helping shluchim and Chabad Houses run their office and systems - and out of my own experience as a shlucha, where I learned firsthand how easily a donation, a goal, or an important date can fall through the cracks.

So I built the tool I wish I'd had back then. This year, after two years of it being just a page, it's finally a real app.

Frequently asked questions

Is my data safe?

Yes. Kivun keeps everything on your device only - no server, no cloud, no third party. It works without internet too.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Through your subscription settings in the App Store or Google Play - exactly like any other subscription on your phone. No need to contact us.

What devices does it work on?

iPhone and Android, on a reasonably current OS version. There's no desktop version.

Does this replace my Google Calendar?

It doesn't replace it - it connects to it. You can sync your whole year straight into Google Calendar and keep using it exactly as you do now.

What happens if I switch phones?

Your planning is backed up. Restore the backup on your new device and pick up exactly where you left off.
